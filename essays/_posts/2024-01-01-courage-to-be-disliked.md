- This book is a distillation of Adler's philosophical and psychological ideas and teachings
- The book has a narrative dialogue between a philosopher and a young man

# Introduction

- The book starts off with the disagreement about the world's complexity. The philosopher argues that it is simple, whereas the young man says it is complex.
- Beautiful example: the temperature of well water. It is always at 60 degrees, but the perception differs based on if it is winter or summer.
- Another beautiful example: Seeing the world through dark glasses. You might take them off and put them back on. But can you even take them off?

> There is no escape from your own subjectivity.

- The issue is not about how the world is, but how you are.

# Deny Trauma

- Greek philosophy - Socrates, Plato, Aristotle
- Austrian psychiatrist - Alfred Adler
- Other psychologists - Freud and Jung

# Why people can change

- Example: someone with social anxiety that is unable to go out because of childhood trauma
- If past events were deterministic of future state, everyone with the same kind of trauma would respond in the same way.

> If we focus only on past causes and try to explain things solely through cause and effect, we end with "determinism". Because our present and future have already been decided by the past, they are unalterable.

- **Teleology**: Everyone is acting according to the goal they have decided for themselves. The goal could be dysfunctional.

- Teleology: Study of the purpose of a given phenomenon, rather than the cause
- Etiology: Study of causation

# Trauma does not exist

- In Alderian psychology, trauam is definitely denied.
- Freud's idea is a persons' psychic wounds cause his present unhappiness.
- "No experience can be cause of success or failure. We make out of our trauma whatever suits our purpose."
- We are not determined by traumas but the meaning we give them is self-determining.
- Example revisited: social anxiety kid's goal might be attention or to avoid becoming faceless or "normal".

# People fabricate anger

- Anger or rage is a choice to fulfil a goal; you create the emotion of anger.
- Anger is not an impulsive emotion, but a tool that can be taken out when needed.
- It is not like you cannot control anger. You use it to overpower someone and assert your opinion.

# How to live without being controlled by the past

- People are not controlled by emotion, or the past
- Freudiean etiology denies free will

# Socrates and Adler

- The first step to change is knowing.
- Plato put Socrates teachings into existence by writing about them; but Socrates never wrote. He and Adler both liked small discussion groups and having public debates

# Are you okay just as you are?

- > The important thing is not what one is born with but what one makes of that equipment.

# Unhappines is something you choose for yourself

- Every criminal has an internal justification for a crime.
- You judged that 'being unhappy' to be good for you.

# People always choose not to change

- Personality and disposition: lifestyle (in Alderian psychology)
- Lifestyle is something you choose for yourself
- Your first lifestyle was probably unconscious - based on external factors - race, nationality, culture and home environment
- Still, it is you who chose "this kind of self"
- This happens around the age of ten
- If lifestyle is not something you were naturally born with, it should be possible to change it over again
- When we try to change our lifestyles, we put our great courage to test. There is the anxiety generated by changing, and the disappointment attendant to not changing.
- Adlerian psychology is the psychology of courage. Your unhappiness cannot be blamed on your past or your environment. You lack the courage to be happy.

# Your life is decided here and now

- People who don't take action don't want to risk failure. He wants to risk the possibility that "I can do it if I tried".
- Having simple tasks that should be done, and coming up with ways to not do them continuously - is a hard way to live.

# Why you dislike yourself

- It is impossible not to get hurt in interpersonal relationships
- As you strive towards different things, thinking the next thing will make you happy - when you achieve it, you find that it doesn't change anything at all.
- If you are afraid of being negated by other people, your goal is to not get hurt by other people. How do you realize this goal? Find your shortcomings, dislike yourself and become someone who doesn't enter into interpersonal relationships.

# All problems are interpersonal relationship problems

- Being alone doesn't make you lonely.
- Loneliness is having people around and feeling excluded.
- Only in social context does a human become an "individual".

# Feelings of Inferiority are Subjective Assumptions

- `Minderwertigkeitsgefuhl` is a feeling (gefuhl) of having less (minder) worth (wert)
- Feeling of inferiority has to do with one's value of judgement of oneself
- Value is based on social context; even a one-dollar bill has no value, except in social context

# Inferiority Complex is an excuse

- Pursuit of superiority and feelings of inferiority are not unnatural but stimulants to normal healthy growth
- An inferiority complex is when people lose the courage to move forwards and make a change towards progress, to improving their situation
- `Complex` - abnormal mental state made up of a complicated group of emotions and ideas, that has nothing to do with the feeling of inferiority
- The complex is used as an excuse
- "Apparent cause and effect": I am not good looking so I cannot get married.

# Braggarts have feelings of inferiority

- Feelings of inferiority are required for growth. They are a feeling of lack and the healthy way to handle it is to strive for growth.
- However, feelings of not being able to grow - are an excuse. It helps you avoid the fact that you aren't what you desire to be.
- Power by association is borrowed power. It means you are living life according to someone else.
- Bragging about your misfortune is a way of manipulating others.

# Life is not a competition

- Adler's concept is life is a flat ground, and everyone is moving forward. There are people moving forward, and there are more people moving forward behind them.
- A healthy feeling of inferiority comes from comparing one to one's ideal self. Not others.
- When you are trying to be yourself, competition will always get in the way.

# You are the only one worrying about your appearance

- If you start to see everyone as competition, in lenses of victory and defeat, every person around you becomes an enemy.
- You start to think everyone is looking down at you, treating you with scorn, they are all enemies who must never be underestimated - in short the world is a terrifying place.
- If you release yourself from this schema of competition, you will be able to celebrate other people's wins.
- Everyone is a comrade.

# From power struggle, to revenge

- Never be taken in by a power struggle. When a person provokes you, they are tempting you to a power struggle.
- A power struggle will change to a desire for revenge.
- Once it reaches the desire for revenge, your interpersonal relationship cannot find a solution.
- Hence, never get taken in by provocation.

# Admiting fault is not defeat

- Bearing something is proof that you are still in a power struggle. Step down and walk away from it.
- Anger is a form of communication. But communication is possible without anger.

# Overcoming the tasks that you face in life

- Adler made three categories of interpersonal tasks - tasks of work, tasks of friendship and tasks of love. Altogether, life tasks.
- There are two objectives of human behaviour - self-reliance and harmony with society.
- You have the ability to choose that people are your comrades.

# Red String and Rigid Chains

- Adlerian philosophy is about changing oneself, not changing others. Instead of waiting for someone else to change, change yourself.
- When friendship turns into love, there might be a difference in behaviour, in permissions. Relationships in which people continuously restrict each other fall apart.
- If you feel constrained in a relationship, even if there is passion, it cannot be called love. Love is when you think - whenever I am with this person, I can behave freely. One can be in a calm and natural state, without feelings of inferiority or the need to flaunt superiority
- Restriction is an idea based on distrust

# Don't fall for the life-life

- When one shifts responsibility for one's current state to someone else, that's an escape route.

# Psychology of Possession to the Psychology of Practice

- It is not what one is born with, but how we make use of this equipment.
