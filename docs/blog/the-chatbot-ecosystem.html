LLMs are the trend today. But should you invest in an AI-enabled customer service?

  • Here’s a list of good questions to ask before investing in an AI-enabled chatbot to your business:
  • What is the volume of requests you receive? Do you have more than 5 customer service agents?
  • Do you have a remote team or audience? Is 24/7 availability important for your business?
  • What kind of requests do you get? Are they open-ended or contained in scope? Can they be answered with logical flows or do they require human judgment?
  • Do your requests require quick information retrieval from different places that might be a struggle for a human? Is this information in the form of large blocks of text?
  • Do you already have automation set up? What fraction of tickets are currently unsolved by your automation system?
  • How many user-related flows do you have? Do you have multiple onboarding tutorials for your app?
  • Do you have a personal touch based business? Do you have a small number of elite users who value personal connection?
  • Can you envision a chat-first interface as the future of your business? Do you see chat assistants as a brand strategy?
  • Can you risk your chat assistant being wrong or incorrect? What is the potential cost of being incorrect?
  • Do you have a high number of inquiries or repetitive tasks? Is your audience diverse and multi-locale?

The best engineering is no engineering. Answering these questions will enable you to determine if investing in an experiment with chat assistants is worthwhile for your business.
