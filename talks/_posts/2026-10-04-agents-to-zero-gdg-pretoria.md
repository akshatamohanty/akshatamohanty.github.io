description: "AI systems don't just fail in production—they drift as real-world data and document formats evolve. This talk demonstrates how to build a practical, continuous evaluation loop on Google Cloud for an AI document-processing pipeline. Through a live demo using Vertex AI, Document AI, and BigQuery, we’ll explore data sampling, human labeling, and performance tracking. Ultimately, you'll learn to create a proactive feedback loop that detects and diagnoses model drift before your customers do."
