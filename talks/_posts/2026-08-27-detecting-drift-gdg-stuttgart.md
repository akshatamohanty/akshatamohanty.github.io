title: "AI in Production: Sampling, Labeling and Detecting Drift Before Your Customers Do"
description: "AI systems don't always fail when they go into production—they drift. Documents change, data distributions shift, vendors introduce new formats, and models behave differently on real-world inputs than they did in testing. In this talk, we’ll build a practical evaluation loop on Google Cloud around a real-world AI document-processing pipeline. We’ll explore how to sample production data, create reliable ground truth through human labelling, evaluate extraction and matching independently, and detect drift before it becomes a customer-facing problem. We’ll walk through a live demo with Vertex AI, Document AI, BigQuery, Cloud Storage, and Cloud Run to create a continuous evaluation and feedback loop. The goal isn’t another accuracy dashboard. It’s a production feedback loop that tells you when your AI system is changing, why it changed, and what to do about it—before your customers do."
